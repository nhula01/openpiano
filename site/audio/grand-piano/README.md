# Sampled acoustic grand piano

FluidR3_GM Acoustic Grand Piano, by Frank Wen and the FluidR3 soundfont team.

Samples were extracted without audio alteration from acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.js
and renamed by MIDI pitch (21–108). Web Audio playback adds volume and release
ramps. Only this piano instrument is bundled, not the complete GM soundfont.
