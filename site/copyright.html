Copyright policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

OpenPiano respects copyright. The built-in library uses only public-domain and openly licensed scores, and the music people import stays private to their own accounts.

Reporting infringement (DMCA)

If you believe material on OpenPiano infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent:

Designated agent: registration with the U.S. Copyright Office in progress
Email: the contact address listed here (being set up)

Your notice must include:

  • your physical or electronic signature;
  • identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed;
  • identification of the material you believe is infringing, with enough detail for us to find it;
  • your name, address, telephone number and email address;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
  • a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

What happens next

We remove or disable access to material identified in a valid notice and let the person who uploaded it know. If they believe it was removed by mistake, they may send a counter-notice with the information required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(g); we may then restore it unless the complainant tells us they have filed a court action.

Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright.

Library sources

Every library score names its composer, transcriber or typesetter, source and license in its notes and in the practice view. Sources include the Mutopia Project and MuseScore transcriptions released under CC0 or the Public Domain Mark (found through the PDMX dataset). Some scores carry Creative Commons Attribution or ShareAlike licenses; the practice data made from them is shared under the same license.

The music in the library is public domain worldwide: its composers died in 1955 or earlier and the works were published in 1930 or earlier, or the music is traditional. If you believe a score should not be here, send a notice as described above and we will review it promptly.
