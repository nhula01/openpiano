Privacy policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

OpenPiano is built to collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no analytics trackers and no selling of data.

Without an account

Songs you import and your practice checks are saved in your own browser (local storage and IndexedDB). They never leave your device unless you choose to sign in.

With an account

  • Email address — to send sign-in links and identify your account.
  • Your songs — the files you add, with the title and composer you enter.
  • Practice checks — the level and skill boxes you tick, so they follow you between devices.
  • Age and terms confirmation — that you confirmed you are 13 or older, and when.

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider. Security rules let only you read or change your songs and progress.

Services the site loads

Like any website, the services that deliver it see your IP address and browser details when you visit: GitHub Pages (hosting), jsDelivr (the music engraver and sign-in library), Google Fonts (typefaces) and Wikimedia Commons (composer portraits). Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

Accounts are only for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete a song any time from My songs, or delete all your songs and practice data from the same place.
  • To delete your sign-in record (email address) as well, write to the contact address listed here (being set up). We delete it within 30 days.
  • Browser-only data is removed when you delete the song or clear this site’s data in your browser.

Changes

We will update this page if what we collect changes; the date above shows the latest version.
