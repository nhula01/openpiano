OpenPiano

Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026 (community added)

OpenPiano is built to collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no analytics trackers and no selling of data.

Installed app and offline use

If you install OpenPiano, its service worker keeps public website files and public scores you open in your browser cache so they can load faster or remain available offline. Supabase account requests, profile pictures and private uploaded songs are not placed in this public offline cache. You can remove the cached files by uninstalling OpenPiano or clearing this site’s browser data.

Without an account

Songs you import and your practice checks are saved in your own browser (local storage and IndexedDB). They never leave your device unless you choose to sign in, or choose to send a printed sheet to the optional scanner when one is offered (the page names the service first).

With an account

  • Email address — to send sign-in links and identify your account.
  • Google sign-in, when enabled — Google provides your account identity, email address and basic profile to Supabase. OpenPiano does not request access to Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Your songs — the files you add, any printed sheet (PDF or photo) you attach, the title and composer you enter, and which hand plays each MIDI track.
  • Practice checks — your level and skill self-checks, sight-reading history and earned practice passes, so they follow you between devices.
  • Age and terms confirmation — that you confirmed you are 13 or older, and when.

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider. Security rules let only you read or change your songs and progress.

Music profiles

You choose your profile picture, name, experience, music skills and bio; we do not automatically copy your Google profile. Profile pictures are stored in a private Supabase bucket. You can keep profile details private or explicitly publish a public profile page. When public, the site gives visitors short-lived access to the picture and links the same profile from your comments. Your chosen name is public when you publish an add-on, post a comment or reply, or reveal a note comment, even if the rest of your profile is private. Making the profile private prevents new public picture links and hides its page and skills; a picture link already issued can remain valid for up to one hour. This does not erase names or text already posted publicly.

Add-ons and note comments

An add-on is your fingering and comments for a whole built-in library piece. It is a private draft that only you can see until you choose Publish; then the published copy of the whole add-on is visible to everyone under your chosen display name, and later changes stay private in your draft until you publish again. You can discard draft changes or unpublish at any time. We store the piece, the notes you marked, your text, account ownership and when you published. Comments you post in a piece’s Comments section are public as soon as you post them, under your chosen display name. Note comments on the library pages also start private; if you choose Reveal, that comment becomes public, replies to public comments are public, and other signed-in learners can save an attributed private snapshot that can remain after you make the source private. Your email and Google profile are not shown. We store one helpful vote per account and reports privately for the maintainer. Withdrawn content is retained for restoration and moderation. Administrators can access stored data to operate and moderate the service. Clearly labeled OpenPiano demo bots provide example practice comments and example fingering and are not human accounts. An add-on draft you make while signed out, and the community add-on you choose to show on a score, stay only in this browser until you move the draft to your account or clear this site’s data. Private uploaded scores and files cannot be attached to or published through add-ons.

Community

The community is optional. Joining publishes your music profile and stores your answer to whether you are 13–17 or 18 or older (used only for the protections described in the Terms). We store what you post (text, the piece it is about, practice results you choose to share, photos, YouTube links), who can see each post, your likes, comments, follows, follow requests, blocks, your working-on list, and reports you send. Other people see posts only as you allowed (Public, Friends or Only me); members aged 13–17 have their photos and performances shown to friends only. Reports, blocks, your age group and your email are never shown to anyone else.

Photos are shrunk and re-saved in your browser before upload, which removes camera details such as location. They are kept in a private storage area; the site gets a link that works for one hour, and only for people allowed to see the post.

A YouTube link shows a preview picture from YouTube (i.ytimg.com) when the post is on screen, and the video plays from youtube-nocookie.com when you press play or open it in Reels. Those requests go to YouTube (Google), whose privacy policy applies.

Leaving the community (from your profile) deletes your posts, photos, comments, likes, follows and working-on list. Deleting a post deletes its photo.

Services the site loads

Like any website, the services that deliver it see your IP address and browser details when you visit: GitHub Pages (hosting), jsDelivr (the music engraver, the PDF reader and the sign-in library), Google Fonts (typefaces) and Wikimedia Commons (composer portraits) and, for community posts with a YouTube link, YouTube. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

Accounts are only for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete a song any time from Library → My songs, or delete all your songs and practice data from the same place.
  • Delete any community post from its menu, or leave the community from your profile to delete all of your community content.
  • Replace or remove your profile picture from your profile. Making a profile private prevents new public picture links; existing temporary links expire within one hour.
  • To delete your sign-in record (email address) as well, write to the contact address listed here (being set up). We delete it within 30 days.
  • Browser-only data is removed when you delete the song or clear this site’s data in your browser.
