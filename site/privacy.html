Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

OpenPiano is built to collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no analytics trackers and no selling of data.

Without an account

Songs you import and your practice checks are saved in your own browser (local storage and IndexedDB). They never leave your device unless you choose to sign in, or choose to send a printed sheet to the optional scanner when one is offered (the page names the service first).

With an account

  • Email address — to send sign-in links and identify your account.
  • Google sign-in, when enabled — Google provides your account identity, email address and basic profile to Supabase. OpenPiano does not request access to Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Your songs — the files you add, any printed sheet (PDF or photo) you attach, the title and composer you enter, and which hand plays each MIDI track.
  • Practice checks — your level and skill self-checks, sight-reading history and earned practice passes, so they follow you between devices.
  • Age and terms confirmation — that you confirmed you are 13 or older, and when.

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider. Security rules let only you read or change your songs and progress.

Music profiles

You choose your profile picture, name, experience, music skills and bio; we do not automatically copy your Google profile. Profile pictures are stored in a private Supabase bucket. You can keep profile details private or explicitly publish a public profile page. When public, the site gives visitors short-lived access to the picture and links the same profile from your comments. Your chosen name is public when you publish an add-on, reveal a comment or post a reply, even if the rest of your profile is private. Making the profile private prevents new public picture links and hides its page and skills; a picture link already issued can remain valid for up to one hour. This does not erase names or text already posted publicly.

Add-ons and note comments

An add-on is your fingering and comments for a whole built-in library piece. It is a private draft that only you can see until you choose Publish; then the published copy of the whole add-on is visible to everyone under your chosen display name, and later changes stay private in your draft until you publish again. You can discard draft changes or unpublish at any time. We store the piece, the notes you marked, your text, account ownership and when you published. Note comments on the library pages also start private; if you choose Reveal, that comment becomes public, replies to public comments are public, and other signed-in learners can save an attributed private snapshot that can remain after you make the source private. Your email and Google profile are not shown. We store one helpful vote per account and reports privately for the maintainer. Withdrawn content is retained for restoration and moderation. Administrators can access stored data to operate and moderate the service. Clearly labeled OpenPiano demo bots provide example practice comments and example fingering and are not human accounts. An add-on draft you make while signed out, and the community add-on you choose to show on a score, stay only in this browser until you move the draft to your account or clear this site’s data. Private uploaded scores and files cannot be attached to or published through add-ons.

Services the site loads

Like any website, the services that deliver it see your IP address and browser details when you visit: GitHub Pages (hosting), jsDelivr (the music engraver and sign-in library), Google Fonts (typefaces) and Wikimedia Commons (composer portraits). Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

Accounts are only for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete a song any time from Library → My songs, or delete all your songs and practice data from the same place.
  • Replace or remove your profile picture from your profile. Making a profile private prevents new public picture links; existing temporary links expire within one hour.
  • To delete your sign-in record (email address) as well, write to the contact address listed here (being set up). We delete it within 30 days.
  • Browser-only data is removed when you delete the song or clear this site’s data in your browser.
