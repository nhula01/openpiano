OpenPiano is a free piano practice website run by the OpenPiano maintainer. By using it you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the site.

Free to use

Practicing, the library and importing your own scores are free. Donations are voluntary and never unlock features, priority or content.

Accounts

  • You need to be 13 or older to create an account. You can use the site without an account; then your songs stay in your browser.
  • Keep access to your email address secure; anyone who can open your sign-in links can use your account.
  • You can delete your songs and practice data at any time from Library → My songs.

Music you add

  • You keep all rights to what you upload. You give OpenPiano permission only to store it and show it back to you so the site can work.
  • Your song and score uploads are private to you. OpenPiano does not publish, share or sell them.
  • Only add music you’re allowed to use for your own practice — for example public-domain works, your own arrangements, or files you obtained with permission for personal use.
  • Don’t upload anything illegal, harmful or meant to break the site. We may remove content or close accounts that break these rules or the law.

Profiles, add-ons and fingering

You may upload a picture for your account profile. If you make the profile public, you give OpenPiano permission to display that picture with your public profile and published add-ons; use a picture you own or have permission to use. Add-ons (your fingering and comments for a piece) and note comments start private. When you publish an add-on, post a comment or reply (comments and replies are public when posted), or reveal a note comment, you keep ownership of your text and permit OpenPiano to display it publicly and other learners to apply, save and use it with attribution. Share only your own advice or material you have permission to share. Do not post private information, copyrighted scores, spam, harassment or impersonations. Private score uploads cannot be shared through add-ons. Profile names are chosen by users. Music experience and skills are self-described, not verified qualifications. Helpful votes do not certify accuracy or safety; fingering depends on hands and technique. You can edit, unpublish or remove your own add-ons and comments. Reports are reviewed by the maintainer, who may hide content that violates these rules. OpenPiano demo bots are labeled as bots and provide example practice comments and fingering rather than human endorsements.

Community and community rules

The Community, Reels and member profiles let people follow each other, post text, practice results, photos and links to their own performances on YouTube, like and comment. Joining makes your music profile public. Each post is Public, Friends (people who follow you and whom you follow) or Only me; you can change or delete your posts at any time. You keep ownership of what you post and give OpenPiano permission to store and display it to the people you chose.

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying, hate, threats, or pressure on anyone to share more than they want to.
  • Post your own playing. Link only to performances you made or have permission to share. Do not post photos or scans of copyrighted sheet music, or other people’s recordings.
  • Respect privacy. Ask before posting photos of other people. Do not post anyone’s contact details, address, school or location.
  • Keep it about music and suitable for everyone 13 and older. No sexual content, violence, drugs, spam, advertising or impersonation.
  • Adults and teens. Members who say they are 13–17 approve every follower, their photos and performances are shown only to friends, and only friends can comment on their posts. Adults must not ask a younger member for private contact, photos or meetings.

Use Report on a post, comment or profile, or Block someone, if anything worries you. Reports go privately to the maintainer. Posts with a photo or performance reported by several people are hidden until reviewed. We may remove content or close accounts that break these rules. There are no private messages.

Uploading video clips directly to OpenPiano is not available yet; it will be added as support for OpenPiano grows. Videos you link from YouTube stay on YouTube and are also subject to YouTube’s terms.

The built-in library

Library scores come from public-domain and openly licensed sources. Each score lists its source, transcriber and license; those licenses apply to that material. Practice feedback checks notes and timing; it is not a music examination or certificate.
