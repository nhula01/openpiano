# Minuet in G

Composer: Joseph Haydn
Transcription: kanailloux on MuseScore, released under CC0 1.0
Source: https://musescore.com/user/9713611/scores/3725481 (via the PDMX public-domain MusicXML dataset, https://zenodo.org/records/15571083)

`original.mxl` is the transcription, bundled unchanged. The practice data
(`practice.json.gz`) is engraved from it with Verovio: hidden playback-only notes (0
in this file) are left out, tied notes sound once, hands follow the staff each note is
printed on, and repeats are written out in performed order.
Fingerings are printed exactly as the transcriber wrote them; none are generated.
