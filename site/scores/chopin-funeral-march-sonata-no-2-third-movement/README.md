# Funeral March (Sonata No. 2, third movement)

Composer: Frédéric Chopin
Transcription: Mariano Azubel on MuseScore, released under CC0 1.0
Source: https://musescore.com/user/24343231/scores/4527441 (via the PDMX public-domain MusicXML dataset, https://zenodo.org/records/15571083)

`original.mxl` is the transcription, bundled unchanged. The practice data
(`practice.json.gz`) is engraved from it with Verovio: hidden playback-only notes (0
in this file) are left out, tied notes sound once, hands follow the staff each note is
printed on. Its D.C./D.S. jumps are kept as printed and played once.
This transcription has no fingerings, and none are generated.
