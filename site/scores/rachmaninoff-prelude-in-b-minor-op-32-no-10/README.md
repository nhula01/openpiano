# Prelude in B minor, Op. 32 No. 10

Composer: Sergei Rachmaninoff
Transcription: Psamtik on MuseScore, released under Public Domain Mark
Source: https://musescore.com/user/3177366/scores/2425281 (via the PDMX public-domain MusicXML dataset, https://zenodo.org/records/15571083)

`original.mxl` is the transcription, bundled unchanged. The practice data
(`practice.json.gz`) is engraved from it with Verovio: hidden playback-only notes (23
in this file) are left out, tied notes sound once, hands follow the staff each note is
printed on.
Fingerings are printed exactly as the transcriber wrote them; none are generated.
